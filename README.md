An invented passport spread for the Republic of Talvera — an archipelago nation that does not exist. The data page shows holder Selin Mara Aurel (passport TV 4829137, issued 14 MAR 2024 in Port Talvera, expires 14 MAR 2034) with a portrait, a biometric chip mark, and a machine-readable strip with valid ICAO check digits. The visas page carries her travel history: a Valdoria visitor visa issued 28 MAR 2024, arrival and departure stamps from Valdoria (Port Meridia, 02–19 APR 2024) and Brennwick (Halden Crossing, 11–28 SEP 2024), her return to Port Talvera on 30 SEP 2024, and the endorsement that reveals the place — the Outer Isles may be visited only with a tide permit, crossings follow the tide tables, fee 12 TAL paid. Every stamp is dated after the passport's issue and before its expiry. main.py copies entry.png to /output/entry.png.
